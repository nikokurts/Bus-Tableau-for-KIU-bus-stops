# Bus-Tableau-for-KIU-bus-stops
Project for my university which is meant to approximately tell when the city municipal busses arrive at university stops.

This is a frontend focused project. During the finals I was able to fully write out the scope I had for this project. While it looks (and pretty much is) just very simple thing, for it being one of the first projects made by me and made by me alone makes me feel somewhat proud.

As for how it works: there are two things user may input, what time it is and  on which stop they are waiting for. After this the tableau outputs what time is left until busses, ones which go to the city and ones which go at the opposite direction of train station, stop where user is. Tableau works on estimation, generally there are two "streams" of busses on this route, one stream generally arives near university at times XX:00 and another at XX:30. Since 08:00-22:00 is the workday of busses, tableau has no output for times beyond this limit.
